Hello! I am Yikai Si.

I am an undergraduate student in the **Zhiyuan Honors Program (Physics Track)** at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, currently studying computational neuroscience under the supervision of Prof. [Shanshan Qin](https://ssqinlab.com/author/shanshan-qin%E7%A7%A6%E5%B1%B1%E5%B1%B1/). In fall 2027, I will begin my Ph.D. studies under the supervision of Dr. [Liu Ziyin](https://www.mit.edu/~ziyinl/).

## Research Interest

My research interests span computational neuroscience and deep learning theory, especially representation, training dynamics, and generalization in artificial and biological neural systems.
